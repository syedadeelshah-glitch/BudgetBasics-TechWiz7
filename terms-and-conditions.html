I

Educational Scope & Non-Advisory Simulation Notice

BudgetBasics is an interactive digital literacy platform engineered strictly for student learning, classroom demonstrations, and personal budgeting practice. It provides interactive mathematical models—such as the 50-30-20 rule, weekly spending limit divisions, emergency cushion timelines, and expense audits.

Non-Advisory Clarification: BudgetBasics does NOT offer certified financial planning, accredited wealth management, legal tax advice, or formal banking operations. All figures generated by our calculators are educational estimates and simulations. Learners must exercise independent judgment and seek licensed financial advice for major financial decisions.

Educational Purpose: The platform is designed to teach budgeting principles. It does not replace professional financial or legal advisors.
II

Hypothetical Simulation Disclaimer & Student Responsibility

Every user acknowledges that student allowances, scholarships, campus wages, living costs, hostel rents, and local inflation vary across geographical regions, academic programs, and personal circumstances.

  • The 50-30-20 ratio (50% Needs, 30% Wants, 20% Savings) is a foundational educational guideline, not a mandatory legal prescription. In high-rent metropolitan student areas, users are encouraged to adapt splits (e.g., 60-25-15 or 70-20-10) as taught in our curriculum.
  • Timeline simulations in Module 4 (Savings Goals) calculate mathematical projections based on constant monthly contributions and do not account for emergency market fluctuations or bank fees.
