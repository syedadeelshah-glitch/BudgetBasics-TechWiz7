# 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Let me explain the real problem students face every single day.

Students often operate with limited, fixed monthly funds. 

A student might receive money from their parents at the start of the month, or get a small scholarship stipend.

In the first week, everything feels fine. But small, unnoticed daily expenses begin to accumulate:

* Daily cafeteria coffee and snacks
* Food delivery orders during late-night study sessions
* Commute and ride-sharing fares
* Academic photocopies and textbooks
* Unused digital subscriptions and gaming skins
* Weekend social outings and impulse purchases

By the third or fourth week of the month, the student finds their wallet empty and asks themselves:

**“Where did all my money go?”**

This happens because students are rarely taught practical budgeting frameworks. They lack a simple system to categorize their spending, differentiate between true needs and impulse wants, and maintain a safety cushion for emergencies.

We did not want to build a boring accounting spreadsheet or a complicated banking portal.

Instead, we designed BudgetBasics to be:

**Simple, visual, interactive, gamified, and genuinely educational.**

# 4. COMPLETE CODEBASE & FILE-BY-FILE ARCHITECTURE

Before we demonstrate the user experience, let me explain how our codebase is structured from start to end.

Our application is built using clean, human-readable, vanilla web standards for maximum speed, accessibility, and reliability:

### 1. `index.html`
This is the central Single Page Application document spanning over 2,200 lines of semantic HTML5. It organizes all 8 learning modules, accessible ARIA attributes, modals for the Guided Tour, Knowledge Check Quiz, Search Index, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Sitemap, and the floating BudgetBee Voice AI assistant widget.

### 2. `assets/css/style.css`
Our master design system spanning over 5,400 lines of structured CSS. It defines semantic CSS custom properties for dual Light and Dark themes, typography rules using Plus Jakarta Sans, glassmorphic navigation bars, interactive card states, glowing badges, floating assistant styling, and isolated `@media print` stylesheets that format reports cleanly on a single page.

### 3. `assets/css/responsive.css`
Dedicated responsive media queries ensuring a flawless user experience across smartphones (320px–576px), tablets (768px–992px), laptops (1200px), and ultra-wide desktop monitors. It handles responsive flex stacking, mobile hamburger navigation, touch-friendly tap targets, and responsive tables.

### 4. `assets/js/app.js`
The primary application orchestrator. It initializes our 7-step guided onboarding tour, global currency state switching across 7 international currencies, the `Ctrl + K` instant search modal, dynamic animated number counters, notification toasts, and the infographic modal renderer.

### 5. `assets/js/calculator.js`
The mathematical engine for Module 3. It computes the 50% Needs, 30% Wants, and 20% Savings allocation in real time, renders and dynamically animates the Chart.js Donut chart, calculates safe weekly spending limits, and formats printable 50-30-20 summary reports.

### 6. `assets/js/chatbot.js`
Our flagship AI assistant, BudgetBee (~1,700 lines of clean ES6+ JavaScript). It integrates the browser's native Web Speech API for speech-to-text recognition and text-to-speech voice playback, contains a natural language number parsing engine that translates spoken phrases like *"twenty thousand"* or *"50k"* into numbers, and automates UI controls like scrolling, filling forms, and generating printable invoices.

### 7. `assets/js/expense-planner.js`
Drives Module 5. It manages daily student expense logging, categories, remaining allowance balance deductions, live budget health indicators (Healthy, Caution, Deficit), session storage, and printable expense audit sheets.

### 8. `assets/js/invoice.js`
Drives Module 8. It constructs certified, printable student budget blueprints formatted like official tuition or banking statements, complete with verification QR code stamps and authorized signature lines.

### 9. `assets/js/quiz.js`
Powers the interactive Module 1 Knowledge Check Quiz, complete with scoring algorithms, explanation popups, and dynamic achievement badge awards.

### 10. `assets/js/scroll-snake.js`
Our proprietary mathematical scrollbar tracker. It calculates a dynamic sine-wave SVG path along the viewport height, animating an illuminated tracer, a live percentage progress bubble, and our mascot tracking document progress.

### 11. `data/` Directory
Contains structured JSON data feeds including `budget-tips.json`, `chatbot-faq.json`, `infographics.json`, and `sample-data.json`.

### 12. `assets/images/` Directory
Houses 9 custom, scalable SVG vector graphics including `info-50-30-20.svg`, `info-budget-flow.svg`, `info-challenge.svg`, `info-latte-factor.svg`, and `info-needs-wants.svg`—all mathematically contained so no text or shapes overflow their borders.

# 11. MODULE 2 — NEEDS VS WANTS DECISION PROTOCOL

Next is **Module 2: Needs vs. Wants Decision Protocol**.

This module introduces our **4-Step Mental Pause Decision Filter**, accompanied by an SVG flowchart (`info-needs-wants.svg`):

* **Step 1:** *Is it vital for survival, rent, or exams?* If yes, it is a True Need—pay it immediately.
* **Step 2:** *Do I already own a usable substitute?* If yes, avoid duplicate purchases.
* **Step 3:** *Can I wait 48 hours before purchasing?* This introduces the 48-hour cooling-off pause.
* **Step 4:** *If still wanted after 48 hours?* Buy it guilt-free from the 30% Wants bucket!

Below the flowchart, two dedicated cards clearly contrast:
* **🟢 True Needs (Prioritize):** Rent, groceries, utility internet, transit cards, and required course books.
* **🔴 Discretionary Wants (Pause):** Gaming skins, extra sneakers, takeaway coffee, and unused subscriptions.

This mental pause stops impulse buying before it happens.

# 12. MODULE 3 — THE 50-30-20 RULE CALCULATOR & CHART.JS DONUT

Now we come to one of our flagship tools:

**Module 3: The 50-30-20 Rule Calculator.**

The 50-30-20 framework is the most widely recommended rule for student budgeting:
* **50% for Needs:** Essential living commitments.
* **30% for Wants:** Discretionary lifestyle spending.
* **20% for Savings:** Emergency cushions and future investments.

### Live Demonstration
Let us test this with a realistic student allowance. 

Suppose my monthly allowance is **$1,200**.

I can enter **1200** into the input field or click one of our quick presets ($500, $1,200, $2,500).

The system instantly calculates:
* **50% Needs = $600.00**
* **30% Wants = $360.00**
* **20% Savings = $240.00**

In the center, our interactive **Chart.js Donut chart** animates into place with proportional slices! 

Below the chart, the calculator divides the 30% Wants bucket by 4 weeks, calculating a **Weekly Spending Cap of $90.00**, so the student knows their safe weekly limit.

And with the **“Print 50-30-20 Report”** button, students can generate a certified, single-page summary report ready to share with parents or mentors.
