Preamble & Academic Scope

BudgetBasics (NextGen BudgetBee) is an interactive Single Page Application developed exclusively for educational purposes under the global IT competition Aptech Techwiz 7 (Category 1: Web Innovation Unleashed). Our mission is to empower college, university, and secondary school students with sound financial planning skills while demonstrating the highest standards of digital privacy, ethical AI integration, and zero data-harvesting architecture.

This document formally ratifies our commitment to privacy. As a student or educator utilizing BudgetBasics, you are guaranteed absolute confidentiality, non-tracking execution, and complete sovereign ownership of your budgeting figures.

I

Client-Side Sandbox Architecture & Zero Network Transmission

BudgetBasics operates as a strictly client-side Single Page Application (SPA). All computational operations—including 50-30-20 mathematical allocations, compound savings projections, weekly spending limits, expense ledgers, and printable PDF blueprint generation—occur entirely inside the browser's JavaScript V8/SpiderMonkey runtime engine on your personal device.

No telemetry data, financial figures, stipend amounts, or student names entered into the application are transmitted across network wires to external backend web servers, cloud databases, or third-party marketing brokers.

Student Safety Guarantee: If you disconnect your internet connection while using BudgetBasics, every calculator, flowchart, and budget blueprint generator continues to operate seamlessly offline.
II

Complete Absence of Financial Credential Collection

BudgetBasics is fundamentally an educational simulation and planning platform. The platform explicitly prohibits, avoids, and refuses the collection of sensitive Personally Identifiable Financial Information (PIFI).

  • We do NOT request or accept bank account numbers, debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or online banking PINs.
  • We do NOT require OAuth connections to commercial banking institutions, wallets, or payment gateways.
  • We do NOT solicit government identification numbers (such as SSN, National Identity Card, or Passport numbers).

All numerical values entered by learners are treated strictly as hypothetical educational numbers for financial modeling purposes only.

No Banking Risk: BudgetBasics will never ask for account credentials. It is 100% risk-free for students.
III

Voice AI & Web Speech API Audio Privacy

Our NextGen BudgetBee Voice Assistant leverages the standardized W3C Web Speech API (comprising webkitSpeechRecognition and the speechSynthesis subsystem). Microphone capture is subject to strict operational privacy bounds:

1. Explicit User Activation: The microphone is engaged solely when you explicitly click the voice microphone trigger or toggle the "All-Time Listening" control within the floating assistant widget.

2. Ephemeral Audio Processing: Voice audio is converted to text either directly on-device or securely through your browser vendor's native speech recognition pipeline. Team Tech Hunters does not own, run, or transmit audio to any speech server.

3. Zero Voice Recordings: No audio recordings, voice prints, or acoustic biometric data are ever captured, logged, stored on disk, or repurposed for machine learning training.

Microphone Control: You can mute or disable microphone access at any time via your browser's site permissions menu with zero disruption to manual typing controls.
IV

Local Storage Persistence & Student Data Eradication

BudgetBasics strictly rejects tracking cookies and persistent marketing beacons. We utilize standard browser localStorage solely to preserve quality-of-life preferences between browser sessions:

  • Theme Preference: Storing your active Light or Dark Mode setting (data-theme).
  • Currency Preference: Preserving your selected global currency symbol ($, Rs., €, £, ₹, C$, A$).
  • Onboarding Tour Completion: Preventing repetitive startup tour popovers once you have completed your initial walkthrough.
  • Session Expense Planner Logs: Storing temporary student budget items so you do not lose progress upon an accidental page refresh.

Data Eradication: You maintain absolute sovereignty over this data. You can instantly purge all stored records at any moment by using the "Clear Site Data" feature in your browser's Developer Tools or settings.

Student Data Control: Your data belongs to you alone. Wiping your browser cache permanently removes all saved calculations with zero residual traces.
V

Third-Party Open-Source CDNs & Non-Tracking Assets

To ensure rapid performance, accessibility, and high visual fidelity across global internet connections, BudgetBasics references static, verified open-source libraries:

  • Bootstrap 5.3 & Bootstrap Icons: Distributed via jsDelivr CDN for UI layout and accessible iconography.
  • Chart.js 4.4: Distributed via Cloudflare CDN for rendering client-side canvas donut charts.
  • Google Fonts: Serving the Plus Jakarta Sans typeface.

These Content Delivery Networks serve static JavaScript and CSS code only. No student entries, budget variables, or speech commands are ever shared with or accessible to these asset providers.

VI

Student Rights, Ethical AI & Fair Educational Practice

BudgetBasics adheres strictly to ethical computing principles. In accordance with student consumer protections:

  • No Behavioral Advertising: BudgetBasics is 100% ad-free. We do not monetize student attention or serve commercial sponsorships.
  • No Automated Credit Scoring: BudgetBee provides educational math and explanations; it does not grade, rank, or evaluate your personal creditworthiness.
  • Unrestricted Educational Access: All calculators, infographics, quizzes, and blueprint generators are universally accessible free of charge.
