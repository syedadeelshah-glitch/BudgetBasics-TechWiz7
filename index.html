Impulse Spending Barrier

The 48-Hour Decision Protocol

Before spending allowance on any non-essential item over $25, execute this simple cognitive filter:

  • Step 1: Ask: "Can I complete my degree and stay healthy without this?"
  • Step 2: Add the item to an offline wishlist and set a 48-hour timer.
  • Step 3: If the emotional urge disappears after 2 days, you just protected your savings.
  • Step 4: If you still genuinely desire it, purchase it guilt-free exclusively from the 30% Wants bucket.

Student Privacy & Data Governance Framework

BudgetBasics ("NextGen BudgetBee") guarantees total privacy for all learners. All financial variables, voice commands, and budget blueprints operate locally inside your browser engine.

I
Client-Side Sandbox Architecture & Zero Network Transmission

BudgetBasics operates strictly as a client-side Single Page Application (SPA). All mathematical calculations (50-30-20 splits, expense logs, savings milestone forecasts, and custom budget blueprints) are executed locally inside your web browser engine. Your budget entries are never transmitted across the network to external backend databases or commercial servers.

Student Guarantee: Disconnecting your internet connection does not break any calculator or blueprint generator—everything functions offline.
II
Zero Financial Credential Collection

We never prompt for, store, or process sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or financial credentials. This includes bank logins, credit card numbers, PINs, social security numbers, or real banking tokens. Any financial figures you enter are purely hypothetical student simulations.

No Risk: BudgetBasics will never ask for account passwords or banking logins.
III
Voice AI & Web Speech API Audio Privacy

The integrated BudgetBee Voice Assistant utilizes standard W3C Web Speech APIs (webkitSpeechRecognition and speechSynthesis). Microphone access is activated solely when you tap the microphone button or enable voice listening mode. Audio feeds are converted to text locally or per your browser vendor's secure speech subsystem. We do not record, transmit, or retain raw audio recordings.

Audio Privacy: Microphone access can be revoked anytime via browser permissions with zero loss of keyboard chat functionality.
IV
Local Storage & Data Eradication Rights

BudgetBasics does not use tracking cookies. We utilize browser localStorage solely for convenience: preserving your Dark/Light theme mode, preferred currency symbol ($, Rs., €, £), and your saved student budget blueprints. You may wipe all local storage data at any time via your browser's "Clear Site Data" utility or by clearing the stored budget plans in Module 8.

V
Third-Party Open-Source CDNs & Assets

To guarantee high performance and accessibility, static styling resources and vector icons are served via trusted open-source CDNs (Bootstrap and Chart.js). These CDNs serve static code libraries and do not track individual learner profiles or display behavioral advertisements.

VI
Governance & Contact Information

For questions regarding student data protection or technical architecture under Aptech Techwiz 7, contact Team Tech Hunters via the official Contact Us module or at info@aglsm.com.
